# Compliance

Selling packaged food online in India carries legal obligations that are
enforced. None of this is optional, and most of it is cheap if built in and
expensive if retrofitted.

**This document is engineering guidance, not legal advice.** Have a CA and a
lawyer confirm the specifics for your registration before launch.

---

## 1. Registrations required before selling

| Registration | Needed for | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **FSSAI licence** | Any packaged food sale. State licence for turnover under ₹20 crore. | 2–4 weeks |
| **GST registration** | Mandatory for interstate sales regardless of turnover | 3–7 days |
| **Current account** | Razorpay settlement | 1–2 weeks |
| **Razorpay KYC** | Accepting payments | 2–5 days after the above |

These are sequential — Razorpay KYC needs GST and FSSAI. **Start six weeks
before your intended launch.** This is the real critical path, not the code.

---

## 2. FSSAI

**On the website:** the 14-digit licence number displayed on the footer of
every page and on each product page. Not buried in an About section.

**On the packaging:** licence number, FSSAI logo, and the veg mark (green dot
in a green square) — every spice powder here is vegetarian.

**On the invoice:** the licence number appears on every invoice.

Implementation: `FSSAI_LICENCE_NUMBER` in backend env, rendered into the
footer, product pages, and the invoice template. One value, one source.

## 3. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities Rules)

Every packaged commodity sold online must declare, **on the product listing
page and legibly readable before purchase**:

| Declaration | Example |
|---|---|
| Name and address of the manufacturer/packer | S V Home Products, [full address] |
| Common name of the commodity | Rasam Powder |
| Net quantity | 100 g |
| Retail sale price | MRP ₹130 (inclusive of all taxes) |
| Month and year of manufacture | 09/2026 |
| Best before | 12 months from packaging |
| Consumer care contact | Phone and email |
| Country of origin | India |

This is enforced against e-commerce listings specifically. The current
`Product` type has none of these fields — schema changes needed:

```
products:  manufacturer_name, manufacturer_address, country_of_origin,
           consumer_care_phone, consumer_care_email, shelf_life_months
variants:  mrp_paise (exists), net_quantity_value, net_quantity_unit
batches:   mfg_month, mfg_year   -- per production batch, not per product
```

**Manufacture date is per batch, not per product.** A separate `batches` table
linked to variants, with the admin recording each production run. Displaying a
fixed manufacture date for a product that is made continuously is a false
declaration.

The product page needs a clearly labelled declarations block — not decorative,
not hidden behind a tab that requires a click.

## 4. GST

**Rates** (confirm current rates with your CA — these change):
- Spice powders and masalas typically attract 5% GST
- Mixed or branded preparations may fall at 12%
- HSN codes generally in chapter 0904–0910 for spices

Every product needs its correct **HSN code**. The schema requires it before a
product can be published ([DATABASE.md](./DATABASE.md)) — an invoice without
an HSN code is not compliant.

### Invoice requirements

Every order generates a tax invoice containing: seller name, address and
GSTIN; invoice number (sequential, unbroken, per financial year) and date;
buyer name and address; place of supply; per-item description, HSN, quantity,
rate, taxable value; CGST/SGST or IGST split with rate and amount; total in
figures and words; and the FSSAI number.

**Two rules that constrain the implementation:**

**Invoice numbers must be sequential with no gaps, per financial year.** Use a
dedicated database sequence, not the order number, and never
`Math.random()` — which is what the current
[CheckoutPage.tsx:53](../frontend/src/pages/CheckoutPage.tsx) does for order
numbers. A gap in an invoice series is a question you will be asked in an audit.

**CGST/SGST versus IGST is determined by place of supply.** Same state as the
seller (Karnataka) → CGST + SGST split equally. Different state → IGST at the
full rate. This is computed from the shipping state, so it must be captured
before the invoice is generated. Getting this wrong means filing corrections.

Invoices are generated by a worker job, stored in R2, linked from the order,
and emailed to the customer. Retention: **8 years**.

## 5. Consumer protection

The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules require, before purchase:

- Total price with all charges broken out — no fees revealed at the final step
- Expected delivery timeline
- Return, refund, and cancellation policy
- Grievance officer name and contact, with a defined response time
- Seller's legal name and address

### Required policy pages

| Page | Must state |
|---|---|
| Terms & Conditions | Governing terms, jurisdiction |
| Privacy Policy | What is collected, why, retention, rights (DPDP) |
| Refund & Cancellation | Window, condition, refund timeline, who pays return shipping |
| Shipping Policy | Areas served, timelines, charges |
| Grievance Redressal | Officer name, email, phone, response SLA |
| Contact | Full business address |

All linked from the footer and from checkout. **Razorpay requires a live
refund policy URL as part of KYC** — this is a launch blocker, not a
nice-to-have.

Food-specific reality: perishable goods are ordinarily non-returnable, but you
**must** accept returns for damaged, expired, or wrong items. Say so plainly.
A policy that reads as "no returns ever" is unenforceable and generates
disputes.

## 6. DPDP Act 2023

Covered operationally in [SECURITY.md §4](./SECURITY.md). The engineering
obligations:

- Collect only what fulfilment needs
- Consent for marketing separate from the transaction, never pre-ticked
- **Erasure on request**, implemented as anonymisation — strip PII, retain the
  financial record for the GST-mandated 8 years. Build it as an admin action.
- No PII in logs
- Breach notification to the Data Protection Board and affected users

## 7. Where each obligation lives in the system

| Obligation | Implementation |
|---|---|
| FSSAI number | Env var → footer, product page, invoice |
| Legal Metrology declarations | New product/variant columns → product page block |
| Manufacture date, best before | `batches` table, per production run |
| HSN code | `products.hsn_code`, required to publish |
| GST split | Computed from shipping state at invoice generation |
| Sequential invoice numbers | Dedicated DB sequence, per financial year |
| Invoice PDF | Worker job → R2 → emailed, 8-year retention |
| Policy pages | Routes in `frontend/`, linked from footer and checkout |
| Grievance officer | Contact page + footer |
| Data erasure | Admin action → anonymise, preserve financials |
| Marketing consent | Separate unticked checkbox, stored with timestamp |

## 8. Pre-launch checklist

**Registrations**
- [ ] FSSAI licence granted, number on hand
- [ ] GST registration active, GSTIN on hand
- [ ] Current account open
- [ ] Razorpay KYC approved

**Product data** — per SKU
- [ ] HSN code assigned and GST rate confirmed with a CA
- [ ] Net quantity, MRP, shelf life recorded
- [ ] Manufacturer name and address, consumer care contact
- [ ] Batch recording process agreed with the kitchen

**Site**
- [ ] FSSAI number in the footer and on every product page
- [ ] Legal Metrology block on every product page
- [ ] All six policy pages live and linked from footer and checkout
- [ ] Grievance officer named with a response SLA
- [ ] Price breakdown fully visible before payment
- [ ] Marketing consent separate and unticked

**Invoicing**
- [ ] Sequential invoice numbering, tested across a financial-year boundary
- [ ] CGST/SGST versus IGST correct for both in-state and out-of-state orders
- [ ] Invoice reviewed and signed off by a CA
- [ ] 8-year retention configured

**Do not launch without the registrations and the policy pages.** Everything
else can be improved in the weeks after; these two carry legal and payment-
processing consequences on day one.
